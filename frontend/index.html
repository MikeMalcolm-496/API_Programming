How claiming works

  1. 1

    Find your item

    Filter by category or search a keyword. You don’t need an account to browse.

  2. 2

    Submit a claim

    Log in with your student account, then describe what makes the item yours. Visit the Security Office if you need an account.

  3. 3

    Get verified

    Security staff review your claim and message you on the item page if they need more detail.

  4. 4

    Collect it

    Once approved, pick the item up from the security office with your student ID.
