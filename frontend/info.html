About the platform

Lost & Found is run by Strathmore University’s security staff. When someone hands in an item, staff log where and when it was found. Students can then search the list and claim what’s theirs.

To protect owners, listings show the category, place and description but keep identifying details back. That is why a claim asks you to describe the item yourself.

Common questions

Do I need an account to browse?

No. Anyone can browse. You only need a student account to submit a claim.

What should I write in my claim?

Anything only the owner would know: brand, colour, marks or damage, what’s inside, and where you last had it.

My claim was rejected. What now?

You can submit a new claim with more detail. Staff may also message you on the item page.

My item isn’t listed.

Items are added as staff receive them. Check back soon, or contact the security office using the details above.
